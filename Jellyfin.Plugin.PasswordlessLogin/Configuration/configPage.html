Passwordless Login

Lets users that have no password log in with a single click on the login screen, as in Jellyfin 10.x. Only applies to requests from the local network and to users authenticated by the built-in provider (not LDAP, SSO, etc.).

Security warning

  • Anyone on your local network (including guests on your Wi-Fi) can open these accounts without typing anything.
  • The login screen will reveal which visible accounts have no password.
  • Passwordless accounts were already reachable with an empty password; this plugin only makes it a one-click action again.
  • Behind a reverse proxy, make sure the proxy is listed in Networking > Known proxies, otherwise every request may look local (or remote).
  • Only give a passwordless account to users with limited permissions (no administration, no deletion, parental controls as needed).
Disabled by default. Takes effect immediately, no restart needed.
